## Why it stopped

Initial cliff flags were all true (no surface detected). The left flag changed
true to false: a surface was detected, rather than a new cliff. Left signal
was 29 initially, 89 on the last healthy sample, 169 at the fault, and 1005 in
the final cleanup sample. Other cliff flags stayed true. Both wheel-drop bits
stayed set (0x0c), Full mode remained active, and no overcurrent, charger source,
button or bumper was reported. Stop was verified and cleanup had no errors.
The box, lighting or small chassis movement could explain the optical change;
these records do not establish which. The bench's cliff-pattern equality rule
caused the abort, rather than a motor compliance failure.

Build 3.1.pit.3 records cliff transitions without aborting. Both drive-wheel-drop
bits remain mandatory; losing either still stops immediately. Normal Roomberry
floor safety is unchanged. This directly addresses nuisance optical changes
while the wheels remain suspended.
